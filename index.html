01Research · Jun 2026–present

Psychedelic Vision Study

Can a frozen Stable Diffusion model turn a forest-road photo into organized geometry, then recover the road?

I gave Stable Diffusion 1.5 the same forest-road photo and caption at every frame. As a simulated dose rose, I reduced the weight of both inputs and increased denoising strength, gain in the first U-Net block, and feedback from the previous frame. I ran the same 40-frame schedule with three starting seeds.

Returned
All three seeds passed both return tests. Mean pixel error was 0.0277 and mean fine-detail match was 0.846 against each seed’s first render.
Repeated
All three peaks developed repeating texture. The mean spectral bump was 1.80 versus 0.44 for the photo.
Failed
The peaks fell short of the geometry threshold, showed little symmetry, and lost motion when I held the settings steady.

02Security research

The server let the client tell it what happened.

Allusion · Jul 2025–Mar 2026 · ~10 hrs/weekClient-side research on Rivals, a competitive Roblox shooter.

No.What the client controlledServer-side fix I reported
  1. F.01
    Anti-cheat reachable from the clientWhat the client controlled: Its report functions sat in the garbage collector, so a modified client could intercept its own reports.
    Server-side fix: Treat client anti-cheat as a speed bump; the checks that matter run on the server.
  2. F.02
    Hit detection trusted a client raycastWhat the client controlled: Redirecting one shared function landed shots the camera wasn’t aimed at.
    Server-side fix: Recompute hits on the server from its own positions, with a lag allowance.
  3. F.03
    Wall check worked aroundWhat the client controlled: Shifting the shot origin on the y-axis got past the shoot-through-walls check.
    Server-side fix: Derive the line of fire on the server and run the wall check there.
  4. F.04
    Body rotation set by the clientWhat the client controlled: The client decided which way its character faced, without tripping flags.
    Server-side fix: Cap how fast and how far rotation can change between updates.
  5. F.05
    Firing input automatableWhat the client controlled: Nothing told a real trigger pull from a scripted one.
    Server-side fix: Flag reaction times and shot intervals that are too consistent.
  6. F.06
    Every client knew where everyone wasWhat the client controlled: Positions and health reached all clients, even behind walls.
    Server-side fix: Interest management: only send what the player can see.
All six reported to the Rivals developers and patched before the research went publicRead the write-up ↗
